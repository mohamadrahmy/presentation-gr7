Conceptual Framework

Meaning and Concept of FDI

Foreign Direct Investment

Lasting Interest: ≥10% ownership and control

Commitment: Fixed factories, land, jobs

Portfolio Investment

Passive Stake: Shares and government bonds

High Mobility: Rapid short-term capital exit

Greenfield

New factories or hotels

Brownfield

Acquiring existing local firms

Horizontal FDI

Same operations abroad

Vertical FDI

Supply chain stages abroad

Factors Affecting FDI in Sri Lanka

✔ Positive Influences ⚠ Negative Influences
Strategic Indian Ocean port location Frequent policy and tax changes
Educated, English-speaking workforce Small domestic consumer market
Ports, airports, expressways, zones Power and infrastructure bottlenecks
Trade access (GSP+, regional FTAs) Technical and digital skill gaps
BOI incentives and sector potential Governance and bureaucratic delays
